## LinkedIn launch post

Most teams do not fail because they cannot build—they fail because they commit before the decision is clear.

Foundry 1.0 is an AI-native Venture Operating System for improving product judgment before engineering begins.

It helps teams move from an uncertain idea to:

- clearer customer understanding;
- visible stakeholder trade-offs;
- research that surfaces contradictions;
- an explicit Proceed, Pivot, or Kill recommendation;
- an execution-ready build plan; and
- traceable engineering artifacts.

The principle is simple: AI should improve product judgment, not replace it.

Every stage should leave behind evidence. Every important decision should remain explainable. And when the team decides to proceed, the reasoning should travel with the work into engineering.

Foundry was built from one question:

**How can we improve product judgment before engineering begins?**

I would genuinely value thoughtful feedback from Product Managers, Founders, and Engineering Leaders.
